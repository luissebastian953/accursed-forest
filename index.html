Sawit Simulator

Turn wild land into a working oil-palm estate: clear, plant bibit, wait out the immature years, harvest TBS on a rotation, sell through your Kopdes — and live with what your clearing choices invite: fires, pests, landslides, and the letters from the district office.

▶ Play in your browser

Runs on WebGPU with a WebGL 2 fallback. Desktop recommended; saves live in your browser.

How a run goes

Clear and plant Chop forest for timber — or burn it, cheaper and faster, and watch the fire pressure meter. Plant palms, fertilise, irrigate, and wait about a year and a half for the first fruit.
Harvest and sell A round every six days once the palms bear. The Kopdes buys at the day's price; upgrade it, widen its range, hire a crew to pick for you.
Live with the land El Niño years, droughts, floods, Ganoderma and rhinoceros beetles, landslides where the forest was stripped, lightning that starts fires nobody lit.
Answer for it Burn and the letters come. Integrity, attention, investigations, an operating ban — or a certificate, if the estate earns one.

How it ends

ISPO certified
Profit, twenty bearing hectares, five years without a burn, forest on the slopes, a full Kopdes — and the President's motorcade pulls up to the door.
Reboisasi
Put more land back to forest than you hold in palms. The ending nobody planned for.
Bankrupt, shut down, arrested
The bank stops waiting, the ban outlasts the cash, or the second wildfire brings the police truck. Rewind to any earlier year and try different decisions.
Twenty-five years
Survived every season, never certified. Keep playing in sandbox.

Controls

Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, Q/E rotate. Click a block, double-click to focus it. Space pauses; 1 2 3 set the speed (a day is ten seconds at 1×). F jumps to the Kopdes, K opens its shop, N the news, ? this list in game.
